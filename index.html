Lesson 1 · Pointer fingers

Every key plays the next note

Rest your pointer fingers on F and J.

  • Keep a steady beat, like footsteps. Step, step, step.
  • The faint star shows when the next note is due. Press the key as it reaches the glowing line.
  • Steady keys make big, bright stars. Pressed a wrong key? Keep going.
or press Space

Made a mistake? Keep going. Press Esc to start the song over.
